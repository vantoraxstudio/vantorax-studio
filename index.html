What to know before you choose

Payment
50% deposit to begin; remaining balance due before final handover.
What “pages” means
Page limits count separate website pages (URLs), not the number of sections on a single page.
Revision rounds
A round is a set of reasonable changes within the agreed scope. New pages, major new functionality and out-of-scope redesigns need a separate quote.
Not included
Domain registration, hosting fees, premium assets, paid third-party services and additional work, unless agreed in writing.

Optional maintenance

From $39 to $79 per month, depending on scope. Covers agreed minor content updates and basic support only, not unlimited work. It is separate from the one-time packages above.
